Product vision and status

Confirmed: Clock Sanctuary is a new, separate clock-themed meditation app for Ahsan Ali (totally blind, iPhone 11, VoiceOver, British English). Sound-only HTML with headings for flick navigation. The spine is the grandfather clock (tick, pendulum, hour strike), with optional weather, soft music, and a low “factory calm” hum.

Status: Design and sourcing only as of 9 November 2026, Europe/London. No app build in this task. A separate builder may start next. Night Fire at /workspace/nightfire was not modified (another worker may be adding clocks there).

Accessibility and product shape

Confirmed: Sound-first; semantic headings; British English; Credits screen for every CC BY asset.

Proposal: Match Sound Sanctuary / Night Fire patterns — large buttons, labelled sliders with spoken percentages, live status text, “describe current mix”, sleep timer with remaining time on request. Prefer offline packs. Do not rely on haptics.

Open question: Safari tab only for v1, or also Add to Home Screen? (Safari-first is safer on iOS.)

Carry-over tone features

Confirmed to carry from Sound Sanctuary / Night Fire: binaural layers; isochronic layers; solfeggio; Tibetan bowls; crystal bowls; didgeridoo; bells/gongs; Journey beat drift (15 Hz down toward 0.5 Hz and back); pink and brown noise; vinyl crackle (confirm which live build exposes it — unfinished verification).

Honesty: Binaural, isochronic and solfeggio effects lack strong, consistent clinical evidence. Present as relaxation sound design, not medicine.

Up to ten layers of each: Wishlist expansion. Twenty oscillators are cheap; many simultaneous beats are muddy and battery-heavier on iPhone 11. Proposal: allow ten in the data model; default 1–2; warn above four.

Multi-point holds in Journey mode

Confirmed key feature for Clock Sanctuary Journey: several hold stages on descent and ascent (not only one hold).

Proposed data model: an ordered list of stages. Each stage has target Hz, transition duration, transition shape (smooth or stepped), and hold duration. Total time is the sum of transitions and holds. Optional spoken stage announcements (default off for sleep). VoiceOver editing: add, remove, reorder, edit fields, speak whole journey and total time. Presets proposed (classic single hold, deep staircase, nap) but not settled.

Clock-linked proposals (not confirmed): tick tempo easing with the journey; hour chime marking stage changes.

Open: reverse ascent vs independent ascent list; announcement defaults; chime on phone hour vs stage vs both.

Feature wishlist — Ten-tone binaural and mood presets

Wishlist / luxury — not core. Full shared note: /workspace/sound-sourcing/wishlist-ten-tone-binaural.md (also for Night Fire / Sound Sanctuary later; do not race the Night Fire worker).

Ten-tone binaural preset: one indulgence button starts up to ten binaural pairs (default beats 2–20 Hz even steps; proposed carriers harmonic series 110–605 Hz). Per-pair VoiceOver volume sliders labelled with beat and carrier; master; mute all; soft fade-in; headphones required; gain ~1/N plus limiter. Ten beats blur — atmospheric chord of pulses, not ten therapies.

Mood presets (Proposal) on the same engine — subset of ≤10 pairs, editable, custom moods savable:

  • Stay Calm — steady alpha/theta (e.g. 6, 8, 10 Hz); no drift; 20–45 min or open-ended.
  • Reduce Anxiety — gentle descent via Journey holds (e.g. 14+12 → 10+8 → 6+4); not stimulating; 15–30 min. Not a treatment for anxiety.
  • Also sketched: Focus; Sleep Onset; Deep Relaxation; Alertness (beat sets, carriers, volumes, drift, durations in the wishlist file).

Mood band labels are conventional associations only — not medically proven.

ADVISORY — Lock screen and background playback

Prominent advisory (from Night Fire / Sound Sanctuary research and practice):

  • Web Audio live mixes (oscillators, buffer engines) often stop or suspend when iPhone Safari is locked or backgrounded.
  • HTML audio elements, optionally with the Media Session API, can continue as ordinary media and may show lock-screen controls — re-test on iPhone 11.
  • Generated tones under lock: pre-render a seamless loop or session file (OfflineAudioContext), then play through HTML audio — as in Sound Sanctuary’s “Make lock-screen track” pattern. Live Journey announcements will not run while locked unless re-architected.
  • Installed PWAs are not automatically better for background audio on iOS.

Honest copy (Proposal): keep Safari open for the full live mix; use a lock-screen track for sleep with the screen off.

Open: is lock-screen support required on day one?
