Underwater water-only sound sources

Sourcing and research only. No app was changed. Prepared for Ahsan Ali. Status date: 9 November 2026, Europe/London.

Goal: Creative Commons underwater ambience with no dolphins, no whale song, no marine mammal vocalisations, and no other animal calls — pure water beds such as waves heard underwater, currents, bubbles, deep-sea drones, and reef texture. Snapping shrimp are flagged as an animal source so you can decide.

Preferred sources: Freesound, Pixabay, BigSoundBank. CC0 preferred. CC BY is fine with credit. Avoid NC because you may start a business. Pixabay uses its own Content License, not Creative Commons — see the Pixabay section.

Where your dolphin tracks already live

These water-only beds are meant to sit beside the dolphin material you already love, not replace it. Nothing was changed in the apps.

  • Live app: Sound Sanctuary v2 at Sound Sanctuary on sharehtml. Notes: /workspace/brainwave/NOTES-v2.md.
  • Dolphin accent layer: key dolphins. Source: Atlantic Spotted Dolphins off the coast of La Gomera, Canary Islands by geraldfiebig — Freesound dolphin recording 385796. Encoded as /workspace/brainwave/enc/dolphins.mp3 and enc2/dolphins.mp3. Embedded on the main Sanctuary page with bowl and boat.
  • Existing underwater bed: key underwater. Source: Underwater Loop AMB by DCSFX — Freesound underwater loop 366159 (CC0). Lives in pack2 with birds, crickets, fire and train. Encoded as enc/underwater.mp3 and enc2/underwater.mp3.
  • Preset that mixes both: Underwater calm uses the underwater bed plus the dolphins accent.
  • Night Fire: no dolphin or underwater layers found under /workspace/nightfire. Water-only beds are a Sound Sanctuary concern (and optionally Clock Sanctuary later), not Night Fire.

Suggested placement (design only): keep dolphins as an optional accent; add new pure-water beds as alternate nature beds, or a Water only / no animals variant of Underwater calm that leaves dolphins at zero.

Top three picks

  1. Kinoton — Underwater Ambience (393819). About 5 minutes 11 seconds, CC0 stereo bed with low rumble, bubbling and light movement. Highly rated. No mammal tags. Best all-round water-only bed to pair with, or stand beside, the existing DCSFX bed when you want no animals. Open Kinoton underwater ambience on Freesound.
  2. felix.blume — Sea waves underwater with bubbles (705057). About 2 minutes, real field recording of underwater waves and bubbles near rocks in Peru. CC0. Authentic real-water colour. Mono. Open felix.blume underwater waves on Freesound.
  3. Tim_Verberne — Underwater Ambience loop (482167). Exactly 2 minutes, CC0 stereo. Author built it as an ambient loop from hydrophone recordings plus filtered noise. Clean meditation-bed candidate. Open Tim_Verberne underwater ambience on Freesound.

Honourable mention: DCSFX 366159 is already shipping as your Sanctuary underwater bed and is already water-only. Dolphins are a separate accent file.

Sourcing policy

  1. Prefer Freesound CC0 and BigSoundBank CC0 for water-only beds.
  2. Use Freesound CC BY only when clearly better, and keep Credits accurate.
  3. Avoid Freesound NC.
  4. Pixabay is its own Content License, not CC; OK inside a mixed app scene; not for standalone redistribution.
  5. Keep the Sanctuary dolphins accent; add water-only beds as separate layers or presets.
  6. Before shipping: listen for mammal calls, shrimp crackle if you exclude animals, train or boat, speech, clipping, and loop seams.
